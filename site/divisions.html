How to read this. All papers where an author lists Maastro (OpenAlex's institution record, or "Maastro" in the affiliation text), plus papers where a division leader lists a Maastricht affiliation (). A paper counts for Clinical Data Science when anyone in the CDS group is on it, and for another division when its leader is. Papers with neither are listed as division not identified, with their first and last author. A paper credited to two divisions counts in both rows and once in the Maastro total. When a preprint's published version is also listed, only the published version is kept. Citation counts are OpenAlex's and change over time.

Led by. The last author leads a paper. A paper is Maastricht-led when its last author lists Maastro or one of the Maastricht affiliations above; otherwise it is a collaboration led from outside Maastricht. Papers are labelled with their leader when that is a division leader, or someone who is last author listed in Maastricht on at least papers here.
