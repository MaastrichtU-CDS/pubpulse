SOCIAL POST STUDIO

Good research. Ready to share.

Turn a paper into a clear LinkedIn post and a branded image. Bring a draft from ChatGPT, then make it yours.

How to share a paper on LinkedIn
  1. Choose the paper Search by title, author or DOI below.
  2. Draft it with ChatGPT Copy the prompt, paste it into a new chat and send it. Use the copy button on the code block in the reply, then Paste & import.
  3. Check every claim Read the post and the image headline next to the abstract. Resolve and remove any [check] marks.
  4. Post it Paste the post in LinkedIn, add the card image, and tag the co-authors and Maastricht University, Maastro and Maastricht UMC+.
  5. Add the first comment Right after posting, paste the comment with the paper link. LinkedIn shows posts with links to fewer people.
Before you post
  • The headline says no more than the abstract's conclusion: no "now", no effect on patients or care, no "first" or "breakthrough" unless the abstract says so.
  • Let the first author read the draft.
  • Preprints are not posted.
